# CFOKit for Claude

Keeps the books for your business in [CFOKit](https://cfokit.ai): records and posts transactions,
records bank and card statements, reconciles accounts, and answers what the books say.

The plugin carries the bookkeeper skill. Claude reaches your books through the CFOKit connector:
in **Customize → Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector**, name it **CFOKit**, paste
`https://mcp.cfokit.ai/mcp`, then **Connect** and sign in with your CFOKit account. Claude acts
only in the companies you allow it in, from CFOKit's web app.

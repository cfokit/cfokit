# CFOKit for Claude

Keeps the books for your business in [CFOKit](https://cfokit.ai): records and posts transactions,
records bank and card statements, reconciles accounts, and answers what the books say.

The plugin carries the bookkeeper skill and the CFOKit connector. After adding it, open its
**Connectors** tab, add the CFOKit connector if it says **Not added**, choose **Connect**, and
sign in with your CFOKit account. Claude acts only in the
companies you allow it in, from CFOKit's web app.

The CFOKit logo files. Each SVG is fixed-color, because an `<img>` cannot inherit color.

- `cfokit-lockup.svg`: mark and name in `ink` (#1b2330) with the `ochre` entry (#c98a1b). Light grounds: `paper`, `surface`, white.
- `cfokit-lockup-dark.svg`: the same in dark `ink` (#e8ecf1) and dark `ochre` (#e1b152). Dark grounds.
- `cfokit-mark.svg`: the T-account mark alone, light grounds.
- `cfokit-mark-dark.svg`: the mark alone, dark grounds.
- `cfokit-icon.svg`: favicon and app icon; the simplified mark in `paper` and dark `ochre` on an `ink` tile, radius 8 on a 64 grid. Works on any ground at 16px and up.

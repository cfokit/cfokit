# Code of conduct

CFOKit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent
the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.

## Reporting

To report behavior that breaks this code, email **[conduct contact to add]**. Reports are kept
confidential, and the maintainers will respond to every one.
